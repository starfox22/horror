Adaptation (glass.glb): extracted the single "highball glass" object (node "highball glass_7", mesh "Object_7")
from the 10-glass collection with the author's own node transform, flipped triangle winding and normals so they
face out of the glass (the source mesh was inside-out and relied on double-sided rendering), uniformly rescaled
to 0.12 m height (metres, Y up), base at y=0, axis at x=z=0; replaced the material with a flat glass colour.
Geometry and topology are otherwise unchanged.
