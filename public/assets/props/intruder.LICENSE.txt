The intruder: "Male_Adult_18" and its animations from the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library
https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox (commit 0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9).
Modified for this game: FBX converted to glTF; animations from the library retargeted by bone name (rotations and root translation); clothes, head and hands dyed black (hood, balaclava, leather gloves); a mask added ("White Mask" by shedmon, CC BY 4.0, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/white-mask-c0d85900a7be438c8d2400bb71649d31: fitted to the head, textures resized); textures resized to 1024 px; roughness derived from the specular map.
