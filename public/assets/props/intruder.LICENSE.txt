The intruder: "Male_Adult_18" and its animations from the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library
https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox (commit 0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9).
Modified for this game: FBX converted to glTF; animations from the library retargeted by bone name (rotations and root translation); clothes, head and hands dyed near-black with little sheen (hood, balaclava, leather gloves), grime on the cuffs, dried blood on the right glove ("Blood Splatter" by ExileGL, CC0 1.0, https://opengameart.org/content/blood-splatter, from the GitHub mirror https://github.com/KurtBliss/Karnage (commit 8dfaa32779482b0de927ddcf46be0a18ce523c09): its spray recoloured and laid over the glove); a mask added ("Porcelain Mask" by sleepwalker77 (https://sketchfab.com/sleepwalker77), CC BY 4.0, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/porcelain-mask-596d7fbddfdd4cbab5a81878e1bd7741, from the GitHub mirror https://github.com/LucasTBorges/VideoPlayground (commit d6437ba743d9234fb197d5a1992974369cf1582f): fitted to the head, its eye holes on his eyes; the glaze made non-metallic with a rougher crackle; its blood tear simplified; textures cropped to the face and resized); textures resized to 1024 px; roughness derived from the specular map.
