First-person arm (public/assets/props/drinking-arm.glb):
"Male_Adult_13" from the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library, https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox
(commit 0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9), Copyright (c) 2020 Microsoft, MIT License.
Modified for this game: the right arm only (sleeve, forearm, hand), cut below the shoulder, re-rigged to 18 joints
with identity rest rotations in a wrist-origin frame, finger poses fitted to a glass, textures converted to glTF PBR.
See tools/arm/README.md and docs/DRINK_ASSETS.md.
